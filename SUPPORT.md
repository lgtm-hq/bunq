# Support

Follow the [LGTM HQ support guide][support]. For this project, include the
component (client, MCP or consumer adapter), revision/version, runtime and a
minimal sandbox reproduction. Redact financial identifiers and personal text;
do not attach credentials, raw configuration or unredacted banking logs.

This is a community integration. Account access, suspected fraud and banking
service incidents should go to bunq through its official support channels.
Security vulnerabilities follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md), not public
support issues.

[support]: https://github.com/lgtm-hq/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md
